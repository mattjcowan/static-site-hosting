About

A backyard, a chair, and a lot of patience

Night Sky Field Notes is a journal kept by the observers of a small backyard observatory under a Bortle 3 high-desert sky.

Why a journal?

Because the sky changes slowly and memory changes quickly. Writing down what you saw, and what it took to see it, is how a list of objects turns into knowing the sky.

Every entry is written the same night or the morning after, while the details are still sharp. If an entry helps you find something for the first time, it has done its job.

How this site works

The pages are plain files. The journal, accounts and sign-in are C# functions shipped in the same zip, in a _functions/ folder, and compiled by the host when the site is deployed. They keep their data in a SQLite database in the site's own data folder, which survives every redeploy and rollback.
